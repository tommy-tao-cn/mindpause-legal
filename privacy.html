Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

  • No account. Mind Pause does not require sign-in.
  • No server. Your app selections and usage data never leave your device.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • You stay in control. Revoke Screen Time access or delete the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Mind Pause (“Mind Pause”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that helps you set your own daily screen-time budget and take a short breathing pause before continuing. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Contact: hellofeedbackdesk@outlook.com

2. Information that stays on your device

Mind Pause processes the following information locally, inside the app and its iOS extensions:

  • The apps you choose to monitor, stored as Apple’s privacy-preserving Family Controls tokens.
  • Daily usage totals and pause counts used to show your budget and energy history.
  • Your settings, such as budget length, intervention mode, and reminder preferences.
  • Temporary unlock and pause timers needed to run the breathing pause.
  • Notification counters used to respect the daily notification limit.

This information is stored in the app’s private container and a shared app group container so the app and its extensions can work together. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account identifier.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, photos, messages, keystrokes, or screen contents.
  • No screenshots or recordings of the apps you use.
  • No analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, or advertising data.

4. Screen Time and Family Controls

Mind Pause uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity, Managed Settings, and Shield extensions) only to apply the limits you configure for yourself. Apple’s Family Activity Picker lets you select apps without revealing their names to the app. Screen Time authorization is controlled by iOS and can be revoked at any time in Settings → Screen Time.

Mind Pause is a self-management tool. It does not monitor another person’s device and does not provide parental surveillance features.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Mind Pause Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription; we do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying sales reports through App Store Connect.

You can view, change, or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

6. Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent through a server, and notification permission can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. Because the app does not transmit your data off the device, there is no personal information for us to share. Apple processes App Store and subscription data under its own privacy policy.

8. Retention and deletion

Your app selections and usage history remain on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can remove individual apps from monitoring inside Mind Pause, and you can delete all local data by deleting the app. If you need help with deletion, contact us at the address above.

9. Children

Mind Pause is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because Mind Pause does not collect personal information on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled directly on your device. You can still contact us and we will respond within a reasonable period.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in a future app update.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hellofeedbackdesk@outlook.com.
