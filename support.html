Getting started

  1. Open Mind Pause and complete the short onboarding.
  2. Allow Screen Time access when iOS asks. This is required to apply limits.
  3. Pick one app (free) or up to several apps (Pro).
  4. Choose a daily budget. When it runs out, Mind Pause shows a 30-second breathing pause.

Common questions

Why do my apps appear as “App 01”, “App 02”?

iOS does not share real app names with Screen Time apps. Mind Pause only receives privacy-preserving tokens, so the list uses numbered placeholders. Your selection still works exactly as chosen.

The shield does not appear

  • Check that Screen Time access is enabled: iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps with access, or Settings → Mind Pause.
  • Open Mind Pause once so it can refresh monitoring after a permission change or an iOS update.
  • Make sure the app you are using is in your monitored list and your daily budget has been reached.
  • If you recently changed your budget, allow a few minutes for iOS to deliver the next usage update.

Notifications do not arrive

  • Enable notifications for Mind Pause in iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them.
  • Mind Pause sends at most a small number of reminders per app per day by design.

How do I add or remove apps?

Open Mind Pause → Settings → Monitored apps. The free plan includes one app; Pro removes that limit.

What is included in Pro?

Pro lets you monitor more than one app and unlocks your seven-day energy history. The free plan includes one monitored app.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the paywall (Settings → Plan) and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mind Pause → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period remains active until it ends.

How do I delete my data?

Mind Pause stores everything on your device. Remove apps from the monitored list, or delete the app to remove all local data. There is no server-side account to delete.

Can I use Mind Pause to monitor someone else’s device?

No. Mind Pause is a self-management tool for your own device and does not provide parental surveillance features.
